The day

From your front door to the fireworks

Your family spends the day across Disneyland and Disney California Adventure with private VIP guides, priority access to attractions, and reserved seating for the holiday parade and fireworks.

  1. Morning

    A private car picks up each family at home. Families who add the character breakfast start there.

  2. Late morning

    Your VIP guides meet you and the day begins across both parks, with priority access to attractions throughout. Holiday highlights include Sleeping Beauty's Winter Castle, it's a small world Holiday, and Haunted Mansion Holiday.

  3. Midday

    Table-service lunch in the park.

  4. Afternoon

    More rides, holiday treats, and Disney PhotoPass photos along the way.

  5. Early evening

    Members head to 21 Royal. Families stay with their guides.

  6. Evening

    Families watch A Christmas Fantasy Parade and the holiday fireworks from reserved seating, then ride home. Members finish with dessert on the 21 Royal patio during the fireworks, then ride home.

The members' evening

The members' dinner at 21 Royal

21 Royal is a private residence in the heart of Disneyland, designed for Walt and Lillian Disney to host family, friends, and dignitaries. For one evening it belongs to CEO Life members: one table for up to 12, a multi-course dinner with drinks, and dessert on the private patio overlooking the park during the fireworks.

Our team shapes the evening around connection. We plan the seating around who should meet whom, host the night, and lead a short conversation between courses.

Each dinner seats 12, and seats go in order of registration.

What's included

One price, nothing to settle on the day

Admission, guides, meals, cars, gifts, tax on included meals, and every gratuity are built in.

Every guest

  • Private car service from your door and back, with car seats for children who need them
  • Park Hopper admission to Disneyland and Disney California Adventure
  • A private VIP guide for each party, with priority access to attractions
  • Reserved seating for the holiday parade and fireworks
  • Table-service lunch, plus snacks and drinks through the day
  • Disney PhotoPass photos, with a framed print for each family in a CEO Life-branded frame
  • A welcome gift
  • All guide gratuities and tax on included meals

Members also receive

  • The members-only dinner at 21 Royal, with drinks
  • A keepsake from the evening
  • A private car home after dinner

Families are grouped into private VIP parties of up to 10 people. Families who want guides of their own can book a Private Family Day.
